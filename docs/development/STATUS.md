## Critical pass and limits

Fixed a consent reread race after waiting for the Workspace lock, bound recording intervals to immutable personal consent acts, separated fast call control from transcription, fenced unknown capture, and prevented late native audio work crossing Workspace changes or restarting after stop. Recording status remains visible with controls collapsed. Shared access and adoption boundaries were not relaxed.

No production mock fallback exists. Missing AI/Brave/OpenAI/Resend/Google/LiveKit/storage configuration remains explicit unavailability/Needs Input. Live STT/model quality, TURN/network handoff, provider token revocation and Egress behavior, exact audio exclusion after consent changes, native background/interruption/Bluetooth behavior and two-/three-person acceptance are **unverified**. Unknown historical recording outcomes remain inspectable and require exact provider evidence; no blind restart or fabricated result. Device-local TTS requires an installed supported voice. Human calls start from an open app; incoming calls to a terminated app, push, video, RIMIAM live in human calls, billing and advanced Context inheritance remain outside this scope.

Final Claude Design, hosting/HTTPS, restore/monitoring, physical-device acceptance, mobile signing/distribution and beta behavioral evaluation remain later activation/design/release work.
